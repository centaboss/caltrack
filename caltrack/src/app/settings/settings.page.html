About CalTrack

Camera-based calorie tracking with offline storage and REST sync.

Built with Ionic Angular and Capacitor. Photos use the native camera, are stored with the Filesystem plugin, and haptics provide vibration feedback. Entries are cached locally and synchronized with the CalTrack REST API.
